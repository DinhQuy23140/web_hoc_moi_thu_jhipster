 Test Day Information
 This contains information about your test day. All test takers should read this carefully.
 Information required for the test can be found either on this website or under the heading of "Test Information" at the TOEIC Application Site. The admission ticket is sent out by mail by about a week before the test date. If your admission ticket does not arrive, please refer to " Reviewing Your Admission Ticket Information. "
 If your name has changed since registration (by marriage, for example), you need to contact the IIBC Test Operation Center before the test date.
 On Test Day 
 What to Bring 
 General Notes on Taking the Test 
 On Test Day
 Swiping from left to right reveals additional information.
   A.M. adm. P.M. adm. 
 Check-in  9:25～ 9:55 14:05～14:35 
 Test instructions, sound check  9:55～10:20 14:35～15:00 
 Test start - Test end 10:20～12:20 15:00～17:00 
 Collection of test books and answer sheets 12:20～12:35 17:00～17:15 
 Exit from test room 12:35 (scheduled) 17:15 (scheduled) 
 You will not be admitted and you cannot take the test if you do not check in by the closing time of the check-in.
 There will be no breaks after the closing time of the check-in, and you will not be allowed to leave the test room after the closing time of the check-in.
 After checking in and sitting at your desk, start filling in side A of the answer sheet.
 What to Bring
 *You will not be admitted on the day of the test and therefore you will not be able to take the test if you fail to bring one identification photograph AND one valid photo ID from the list below. Your test fee will not be refunded in such cases.
 1
 Admission ticket
 For information on the scheduled arrival date of the admission ticket, or if your admission ticket has not arrived by the scheduled arrival date, see " Receiving Admission Ticket/Undelivered " for the necessary procedures.
 2
 Identification photograph
 Prepare an identification photograph that meets the requirements , write your name and the registration number on the back, and attach it to the Identification Photograph area of your admission ticket. 
* Your identification photograph will be printed in black and white on your Official Score Certificate.
 3
 Acceptable ID (Valid and original photo ID)
 Acceptable IDs are listed below. 
 Acceptable IDs (Valid and original photo IDs)
 * You MUST bring one of the following to be admitted on the test day.
* Photocopies/photographed images will not be accepted.
* Please note that the rule differs for those who have Japanese nationality and those who do not have Japanese nationality.
 ≪Japanese≫ 
※Only IDs issued in Japan are accepted.
 Driver’s license (Provisional driver’s license / Driving record certificate)
 Passport
 Individual Number Card (My Number Card) ※1 
 Physical disability certificate (Intellectual disability certificate, Mental disability certificate, etc.)
 Student ID (Digital student IDs are also accepted.)
 ≪Non-Japanese≫ ※2 
＊With the exception of passports, IDs issued in countries other than Japan are not accepted.
 Residence Card / Specified Residence Card（with photo）
 Passport
 Special permanent resident certificate / Specified special permanent resident certificate （with photo）
 Individual Number Card (My Number Card) ※1 
 Any digital Individual Number Card (including Mynaportal App) is not accepted. Please be sure to present the original card.
 You will not be permitted to take the test if your name on acceptable ID and your registered name do not match.
Please note that driver's licenses or student IDs are not accepted for non-Japanese nationals. 
 Examples of unacceptable IDs
 * Any IDs other than listed above are NOT accepted. You will not be allowed to take the test if you bring any of the following IDs.
 Employee ID/ Government Employee ID
 Any license other than driver’s license/provisional driver’s license
 Health insurance card
 Certificate of residence
 Certificate of individual records/Certificate of the family register　etc.
 4
 Writing instruments
 HB pencil or mechanical pencil, eraser
 5
 Wristwatch
 There will be no announcements of the time during the test.
You may not use anything other than a wristwatch as a timepiece. This means no mobile phones, desk clocks, stopwatches, or wearable devices.
 Your admission ticket and photograph will be collected at the test center and will not be returned.
