 The Official Learning and Preparation Course for the TOEIC Bridge ® Listening and Reading Tests 
 This online course is designed to help English-language learners at beginner to low-intermediate levels build and improve their English-language communication skills while also preparing them to take the TOEIC Bridge Listening and Reading tests. The course includes:
 Eight topic-based, skill-building units
 Engaging and effective learning activities and practice questions
 Two full-length, authentic TOEIC Bridge Listening and Reading practice tests (with answer keys and rationales)
 Voice narration in the same voices used on the actual TOEIC Bridge Listening and Reading tests
 Automated score report
 An assessment of the course user’s strengths and areas where they can improve
 To order the course, contact your local EPN office .
