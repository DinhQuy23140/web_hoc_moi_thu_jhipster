 Understanding TOEFL Scores 
 TOEFL iBT Scores – All you need to know
 Your TOEFL iBT® scores do more than just measure English, they help you stand out to colleges and universities around the world. Each score provides:</p>\r\n<ul>\r\n<li>A reliable reflection of how you’ll perform in real academic settings</li>\r\n<li>Fair, unbiased evaluation through a secure and centralized scoring system</li>\r\n<li>Global recognition, accepted by over 13,500 institutions in more than 160 countries</li>\r\n</ul>\r\n<p>When your goal is advancing your academic career, choose the test that’s widely accepted and trusted by admissions teams globally.<br>\r\n&nbsp;</p>\r\n"}}" id="text-9d90e174fe" class="cmp-text">
 Your TOEFL iBT® scores do more than just measure English, they help you stand out to colleges and universities around the world. Each score provides:

 Score requirements 
 There are no passing or failing scores set by the TOEFL Program or ETS.
 Each institution sets its own score requirements. For more information about how your scores will be used or interpreted, we recommend contacting the institution directly.
 How the test is scored
 TOEFL tests are only scored by a centralized scoring team, never at the test center.</p>\r\n<p>Our scoring methods:</p>\r\n<ul>\r\n<li>Offer a complete and accurate picture of your ability</li>\r\n<li>Minimize the rater bias that can occur with face-to-face interviews</li>\r\n<li>Ensure consistency and fair scoring standards.</li>\r\n</ul>\r\n"}}" id="text-ac458ae33a" class="cmp-text">
 TOEFL tests are only scored by a centralized scoring team, never at the test center.

 Minimize the rater bias that can occur with face-to-face interviews
 Ensure consistency and fair scoring standards.

 What Changed in January 2026
 As of January 21, 2026, TOEFL iBT score reports feature a new 1 – 6 scale, in half point increments, with a more intuitive alignment to the Common European Framework of References for Language (CEFR).</p>\r\n<p>A few notes on this scoring change:</p>\r\n<ul>\r\n<li>You’ll receive four section scores, as well as an overall score, on the 1 – 6 scale.</li>\r\n<li>The overall score will be the average of all four sections, rounded to the nearest half band (for example, if the average of your section scores is 5.25, you will receive an overall score of 5.5).</li>\r\n<li>For a two-year transition period after January 2026, you will also receive a comparable overall score on the 0 – 120 scale, representing the midpoint of the corresponding total range.</li>\r\n</ul>\r\n"}}" id="text-ac51b11224" class="cmp-text">
 As of January 21, 2026, TOEFL iBT score reports feature a new 1 – 6 scale, in half point increments, with a more intuitive alignment to the Common European Framework of References for Language (CEFR).
 A few notes on this scoring change:
 You’ll receive four section scores, as well as an overall score, on the 1 – 6 scale.
 The overall score will be the average of all four sections, rounded to the nearest half band (for example, if the average of your section scores is 5.25, you will receive an overall score of 5.5).
 For a two-year transition period after January 2026, you will also receive a comparable overall score on the 0 – 120 scale, representing the midpoint of the corresponding total range.

 MyBest® Scores
 If the score report is for a test taken on the updated TOEFL score scale (1- 6), the MyBest Score section of the test taker’s score report will convert older scores on the 0 – 120 scale to the 1 – 6 scale.
 If the score report is for a test taken on the prior TOEFL score scale (0-120), the MyBest Score section of the test taker’s score report will only include scores from the prior scale (0 – 120).

 Frequently Asked Questions
 Where can I find my TOEFL iBT scores? 
 At the end of the test, you'll see your unofficial Reading and Listening scores. Your official scores will be posted in your ETS account 3 days after your test.</p>\r\n"}}" id="text-f3cdd15992" class="cmp-text">
 At the end of the test, you'll see your unofficial Reading and Listening scores. Your official scores will be posted in your ETS account 3 days after your test.
 What is a good TOEFL iBT score? 
 A good TOEFL iBT score for you is the score you need to meet the English-language requirements of the institutions where you are applying Institutions’ requirements vary, so be sure to check the requirements with them directly.</p>\r\n"}}" id="text-d732442cc8" class="cmp-text">
 A good TOEFL iBT score for you is the score you need to meet the English-language requirements of the institutions where you are applying Institutions’ requirements vary, so be sure to check the requirements with them directly.
 How long are TOEFL iBT scores valid? 
 TOEFL iBT scores are valid for 2 years from the test date. ETS will not report or send scores after 2 years.</p>\r\n"}}" id="text-ab3eb6954b" class="cmp-text">
 TOEFL iBT scores are valid for 2 years from the test date. ETS will not report or send scores after 2 years.
 Can I use TOEFL iBT MyBest scores when I apply? 
 Each institution sets its own requirements. Check directly with the institutions where you're applying to confirm whether they accept MyBest scores.</p>\r\n"}}" id="text-28a68e6ab8" class="cmp-text">
 Each institution sets its own requirements. Check directly with the institutions where you're applying to confirm whether they accept MyBest scores.
 Can I get a PDF version of my TOEFL iBT score report? 
 PDF score reports are ready for download 1 day after you receive your scores in your ETS account.</p>\r\n"}}" id="text-3edf7b65e1" class="cmp-text">
 PDF score reports are ready for download 1 day after you receive your scores in your ETS account.
 Can I appeal my TOEFL iBT scores? 
 You can request a score review for Speaking and/or Writing within 30 days of your test, for a fee. You can’t request a score review if you have already requested that your scores be sent to any institution or agency. Reading and Listening scores cannot be reviewed or appealed.</p>\r\n"}}" id="text-98298d0375" class="cmp-text">
 You can request a score review for Speaking and/or Writing within 30 days of your test, for a fee. You can’t request a score review if you have already requested that your scores be sent to any institution or agency. Reading and Listening scores cannot be reviewed or appealed.
 Why are the Reading and Listening scores shown at the end unofficial? 
 All scores are required to go through ETS quality checks before they are officially released. It is possible that the scores could change due to the statistical analysis performed after the test to ensure accuracy.</p>\r\n"}}" id="text-60c6546c9d" class="cmp-text">
 All scores are required to go through ETS quality checks before they are officially released. It is possible that the scores could change due to the statistical analysis performed after the test to ensure accuracy.
 Will my unofficial Reading or Listening score change? 
 It is rare but possible that your score will change after required ETS quality checks. This is why the scores are unofficial until you receive them in your account.</p>\r\n"}}" id="text-0daa90d4e6" class="cmp-text">
 It is rare but possible that your score will change after required ETS quality checks. This is why the scores are unofficial until you receive them in your account.
 Can I cancel my TOEFL iBT scores before an institution sees them? 
 At the end of the test, after viewing unofficial scores, you can report or cancel all scores. If you cancel your scores, you will not receive a refund of your test fee. Canceled scores can be reinstated within 60 days for a fee. Once you choose to report, scores cannot be canceled. If you order additional score reports after the test, once the order is placed, it cannot be canceled.</p>\r\n"}}" id="text-d6d3704586" class="cmp-text">
 At the end of the test, after viewing unofficial scores, you can report or cancel all scores. If you cancel your scores, you will not receive a refund of your test fee. Canceled scores can be reinstated within 60 days for a fee. Once you choose to report, scores cannot be canceled. If you order additional score reports after the test, once the order is placed, it cannot be canceled.
 The university I want to apply to still shows scores as 0 to 120. Can I still apply? 
 Yes. Institutions are updating their admissions pages to the new 1 to 6 format at different times, and some still show 0 to 120. Your official TOEFL iBT score report includes both formats, so you can apply as usual.</p>\r\n"}}" id="text-ecf596a097" class="cmp-text">
 Yes. Institutions are updating their admissions pages to the new 1 to 6 format at different times, and some still show 0 to 120. Your official TOEFL iBT score report includes both formats, so you can apply as usual.
 My score report shows 1 to 6, but the school lists 0 to 120. How do I know if I meet their requirement? 
 Your report includes a comparable 0 to 120 total score estimate alongside your 1 to 6 scores. Compare that estimate against the school's listed requirement. There's nothing you need to calculate or convert.</p>\r\n"}}" id="text-a12b61a6e1" class="cmp-text">
 Your report includes a comparable 0 to 120 total score estimate alongside your 1 to 6 scores. Compare that estimate against the school's listed requirement. There's nothing you need to calculate or convert.
 Do I need to convert my score before I apply? 
 No. Both formats are already on your official score report.</p>\r\n"}}" id="text-62145a17ae" class="cmp-text">
 No. Both formats are already on your official score report.
 What if the institution hasn't updated its requirements yet?
