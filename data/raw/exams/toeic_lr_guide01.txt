 How To Apply
 You will need to become a member and sign in to TOEIC Application Site 
 If you have already created your account as a member, log in to the TOEIC Application Site. If you are not a member yet, click “Create your account (Free)” and carry out the sign-up procedures.
Your account can be used for online applications for both the TOEIC Listening & Reading Test in Secure Program and the TOEIC Speaking & Writing Tests in Secure Program.
 Apply Online 
 Please make sure to confirm the TOEIC L&R Guidelines before you apply for the TOEIC L&R.
 Test Fee/Discount System
 Test Fee
 If you need to receive a printed Official Score Certificate： JPY7,810（incl. tax） 
 If you don't need to receive a printed Official Score Certificate： JPY7,700（incl. tax） 
 A Digital Official Score Certificate is displayed to every examinee.
 Test fees will not be refunded once paid.
 Discount System
 You can take the test six months later at a discount price.
 If you apply for the TOEIC L&R Test in Secure Program, the discounted test fee will be automatically applied to one of the tests to be held between six months after the test date and the same month of the following year. You need to use the same account to get the discount.
If you use this discount service to take the test, the same discount will also apply after the next test date. This is a special service for those taking the test multiple times.
 If you applied for the TOEIC L&R Test from August 25, 2024 to December 8, 2024 and have a valid repeater discount at the time of transition to the new discount service, you will be eligible for a discount from January 26, 2025 to December 20, 2025.
--> This discount service does not apply to group application and makeup test vouchers.
 Payment Methods
 The following payment methods are accepted.
 Credit card
 Visa, Mastercard, JCB
 Convenience stores
 Seven-Eleven
 Lawson, Ministop, FamilyMart, Seicomart
 Other payment methods
 Rakuten Pay
